THREE PARTS THAT MAKE COMPUTER GO BRRR

THE CPU!!!

The processor does calculations and runs instructions. It is basically the brain of the computer, except it does not have a brain.

Buy a good one or your computer might be slower than a toaster.

THE GPU???

The graphics card renders images, games, and other visual stuff. Gaming PCs often use powerful dedicated GPUs.

They can also cost approximately one million dollars. Probably.

RAM!!!!

RAM stores data that your computer is actively using. More RAM can help with multitasking, depending on what you are doing.

RAM is not storage. Please stop putting your files in the RAM.

OTHER COMPUTER THINGS

  • Motherboard: connects the components together.
  • Power supply: supplies power to the computer.
  • Storage: keeps files when the computer is turned off.
  • Cooling: prevents components from overheating.
